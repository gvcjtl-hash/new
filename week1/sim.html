The Fizz-Pop launch

The client

Fizz-Pop

A new cola entering a shelf full of near-identical bottles. Big budget, big ambition, and a first ad that already landed with a thud. Your job this week: get the launch back on one clear track — and make it cut through.

You'll make eight calls to get Fizz-Pop's launch back on track. Here's how the account works:

Your role

You're the new intern. You make each call; Dana tells you how the client reacts.

The metrics up top

Four live numbers that carry across every decision — the shape you leave them in decides how the launch ends:

  • Awareness — how many people know Fizz-Pop exists
  • Message Clarity — how unified and on-point the message is
  • Buzz — engagement and word of mouth
  • Budget — what's left to spend
Each call

Land the strongest move and the campaign gains. Miss it and you can try again; if you're stuck, reveal the reasoning for all four — but then the launch takes no lift from that call. No quiz score — the campaign itself is the result.
