Welcome to MKT 150

Welcome to Principles of Advertising. Before we start analyzing how global brands grab consumer attention, practice on the most important brand you will ever manage: yourself.

Every introduction you share is a small marketing message. You decide what to say, how to frame it, and who you want to reach. That is Integrated Marketing Communications on a personal scale. Notice which classmates' posts catch your eye.

Main Post Prompt

Include all four of the following in your main introduction post:

  • 1. The Basics: Preferred name, where you are logging in from, and your major or target career path.
  • 2. Your Personal Brand in Three Words: If you were a product on a store shelf, what three descriptor words would be on your label (for example: Loyal, Curious, Caffeinated)? You may also include a short personal tagline.
  • 3. The Fun Fact: One interest, hobby, or unique skill that makes you distinct.
  • 4. The Marketing Hook (A or B):
    Option A: An advertisement you cannot forget (loved or hated) and why it stuck.
    Option B: A brand you are loyal to and what keeps you returning.

Example Post

Sample introduction

"Hello everyone! My name is Jordan, logging in from Des Moines. I am a Business Administration major working toward a career in social media marketing. My three personal brand words are Organized, Persistent, and Snack-Driven, with the tagline: 'Gets it done, but bring snacks.' A fun fact about me is that I can solve a Rubik's cube in under two minutes. An advertisement I will never forget is the classic Budweiser Clydesdale Super Bowl campaign. It used no dialogue, relied entirely on emotion, and stuck with me for years. That emotional connection is the marketing skill I am most excited to learn in this class."

Connecting with Classmates

Peer replies are optional for this introductory post, but reading through classmates' posts is a good way to start building connections.

  • Find common ground: Same major, hometown, or hobbies.
  • Discuss marketing hooks: React if someone named an ad or brand you recognize.
  • Ask questions: Follow up on career goals or a fun fact.
